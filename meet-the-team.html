Meet the ITAVCollab Team

Leadership Team

Shoareged Phillpotts, MPH, LMSW

Co-Founder, Executive Director

Shoa is a passionate, purpose driven leader who believes holistic wellness is the key to social change. She leads with a culturally competent, community-centered approach, tackling trauma, systemic inequities, and reintegration through her work so that historically resilient communities can thrive.

Licensed Therapist Integrative Wellness Practitioner Advocate

Aishia Manley, M.A.

Co-Founder, Chief Wellness Advisor

With over 20 years in education, a Master's in Psychology, and certification as an Ayurvedic practitioner, Aishia is deeply passionate about supporting others holistically. As a mother and wellness advocate, she leads community-centered initiatives that nurture the whole person and guide others on their wellness journey.

Educator Ayurvedic Practitioner Advocate

Sydni Turner, M.A.

Director of Healing Justice Initiatives

Sydni is deeply passionate about addressing mass incarceration, the Black family, and reentry. As a published author and mentor, she pours her heart into creating reentry services prioritizing lived experiences, peer mentorship, and advocacy training. Sydni is committed to ensuring that returning citizens have the support and tools they need not just to survive but thrive.

Sociologist Author Advocate
