About ITAVCollab

We envision a world of thriving communities where wellness is accessible, culturally affirming, and sustained across generations.

Founded in 2019, It Takes A Village Collaborative (ITAVCollab) was created by sisters Shoareged Phillpotts and Aishia Manley-Phillpotts, whose Caribbean upbringing in Hyattsville, Maryland, instilled in them a deep commitment to kinship, community care, and shared responsibility.

Raised with the belief that "it takes a village" is more than a proverb—it is a practice—the sisters witnessed the challenges Black women face in navigating health and wellness systems that often fail to acknowledge their lived experiences. What began as a conversation between sisters quickly evolved into a movement.

Through dialogue circles and community listening sessions, Shoareged and Aishia heard recurring stories of burnout, generational fatigue, and a lack of culturally relevant resources for healing and self-care.

These experiences helped shape ITAVCollab's mission: to close critical gaps in holistic wellness services and ensure that community members—across generations—have access to the mental, emotional, and physical support they deserve.

Today, ITAVCollab serves as a trusted wellness hub for residents of Prince George's County, MD, and Washington, DC. We bring together practitioners, healers, educators, and community leaders to provide trauma informed, culturally grounded offerings that center Black women and those justice impacted.

Our vision is bold yet deeply rooted: to build thriving historically resilient communities by nurturing wellness as a collective, intergenerational right and legacy.
