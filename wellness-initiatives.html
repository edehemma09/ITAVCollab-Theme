Wellness Initiatives

Release, Restore, & Reconnect

The Queen B.E.E. Talks Experience

Queen B.E.E Talks is an experience designed to bridge the gap in the wellness space for historically resilient women. In our own lived experiences, we have observed that there was very little conversation around our health and wellness. Through wellness circles, cohorts, and retreats, this experience was created to fill that void.

Wellness Circles

Intimate, facilitated sessions that provide a space for sharing, emotional expression, and collective healing.

Wellness Cohorts

Multi-week supportive, guided groups focusing on holistic self-care, personal growth, and community-care.

Wellness Retreats

Immersive, bi-annual experiences offering rest, reflection, and personal growth, in a rejuvenating environment.
